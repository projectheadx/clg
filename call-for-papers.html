International Conference on Sustainable Solutions in Engineering Technologies and Earth Science (ICSSET – 26) organized by I.T.S Engineering College, Greater Noida (Affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow)

The International Conference on Sustainable Solutions in Engineering Technologies and Earth Science (ICSSET – 26) serves as a dynamic platform for academics, researchers, engineers, professionals, and industry experts to share knowledge and explore advancements in sustainable technologies and earth sciences. The conference aims to promote interdisciplinary research and innovative solutions that address environmental, social, and technological challenges to ensure a sustainable and resilient future.

ICSSET – 26 invites original, unpublished research papers, case studies, and review articles in the following thematic areas (but not limited to):

Conference Tracks

Track 1: Computer Science, AI, and Automation for Sustainability
  • AI, machine learning, and data analytics for green technologies
  • Cybersecurity and blockchain for sustainable digital systems
  • IoT-based Monitoring & Optimization frameworks
  • Edge, cloud, and quantum computing
  • Digital twins and smart automation for resource efficiency
Track 2: Communication, Networking and Broadcast Technologies
  • Wireless Communication Systems
  • 5G/6G Communication Technologies
  • Green IoT and Sustainable Communication Systems
  • Internet of Things (IoT) Systems
  • Network Security and Communication Systems
Track 3: Electronics, VLSI, and Communication Systems
  • Low-power and energy-efficient VLSI design
  • RF, microwave, and antenna technologies for sustainable communication
  • Wireless, optical, and satellite communication systems
  • Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor networks for smart environments
  • Smart Environment Solutions using communication and embedded systems
Track 4: Signal Processing, Computer Vision and Intelligent Applications
  • Digital Signal Processing
  • Image and Video Processing
  • Speech and Audio Processing
  • Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning
  • Biomedical Signal Processing
Track 5: Power, Energy and Sustainable Technologies
  • Power Systems and Smart Grid Technologies
  • Renewable Energy Systems (Solar, Wind, Hybrid)
  • AI for Sustainable Energy Systems
  • Energy Management and Conservation
  • Power Electronics and Electric Drives
Track 6: Robotics, Automation and Control Systems
  • Intelligent Robotics Systems
  • Autonomous Systems and Drones
  • Industrial Automation
  • Control Theory and Applications
Track 7: Healthcare, Biotechnology, and Life Sciences
  • Sustainable Healthcare Solutions and medical technologies
  • Medical image processing
  • Health informatics and AI-based diagnostic tools
  • Biomedical Devices and Sensors
  • Environmental biotechnology and waste utilization
Track 8: Electromagnetics, Materials and Emerging Engineering Technologies
  • Electromagnetic Fields and Waves
  • Antenna Design and RF Systems
  • Advanced Materials for Engineering Applications
  • Plasma Science and Applications
Track 9: Earth Sciences and Geotechnical Innovations
  • Geological Hazard Assessment and Disaster Risk Reduction Techniques
  • Integration of Engineering and Earth Sciences for Global Sustainability
  • Geotechnical and geoenvironmental engineering
  • Hydrological modeling and earth observation
  • Climate change impact analysis and adaptation strategies
