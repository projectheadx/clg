About ICSSETES

ICSSETES is a prestigious international IEEE conference that brings together researchers, engineers, and scholars from around the world to showcase their latest research findings and experiences in computing, communication and networking. Featuring keynote speeches, technical sessions, and workshops, the conference covers a wide range of topics such as cloud computing, AI, wireless communication systems, IoT, and optoelectronics.

About College

ITS Engineering College, located in Greater Noida, is a distinguished institution for Engineering and Management, founded in 2006 by Dr. R.P. Chadha. Committed to delivering high-quality, industry-focused education at an affordable cost. With a rich history spanning over three decades, The ITS Education Group has emerged as a key player in higher education in North India, operating nine institutes with more than 20,000 students and a dedicated staff of over 1,250. The group excels in various disciplines, including Information Technology, Engineering, Management, Dental, Pharmacy, and Physiotherapy. Established in 2006 under the visionary leadership of Chairman Dr. R.P. Chadha, ITS Engineering College holds NBA accreditation for its dedication to providing high-quality education. Approved by AICTE and affiliated with AKTU Lucknow, the college offers diverse programs, such as B.Tech in CSE, Computer Science & Engineering (AI), CSE-Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, CSE-Data Science, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering (IoT, Design and Technology), Mechanical and Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering as well as MBA programs in Marketing, Finance, Human Resource, Operations, International Business, and Information Technology.

About Electronics and Communication Engineering

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at ITS Engineering College, Greater Noida was established in 2006 with the mission to produce technically skilled, innovative, and socially responsible engineers capable of meeting global technological challenges. The department emphasizes quality education, research, and industry-oriented training to prepare students for diverse career opportunities.

The department offers a four-year B.Tech program in Electronics and Communication Engineering, affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow (formerly UPTU). The program is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi, highlighting its commitment to academic excellence, outcome-based education, and continuous improvement.

The department has a team of highly qualified and experienced faculty members, many of whom are doctorates and degree holders from reputed institutions such as IITs, NITs, and IIITs. Their expertise spans across VLSI design, embedded systems, communication networks, signal processing, and automation, ensuring students receive both strong theoretical knowledge and practical insight.

To strengthen industry-academic collaboration, the department has established the NI-Innovation Center in partnership with National Instruments (NI). This center features an NI-based Instrumentation and LabVIEW Automation Laboratory, enabling students to work on advanced technology projects involving hardware-software integration and real-world applications.

Student creativity is further enriched through active technical bodies such as the IEEE Student Branch and the student activity club "e-Vision". These platforms organize workshops, seminars, webinars, technical quizzes, poster presentations, and project exhibitions, encouraging innovation, leadership, and professional development.

With modern laboratories, a research-driven environment, and dedicated faculty, the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering continues to produce graduates who excel in fields like telecommunication, embedded systems, signal processing, automation, and software development, contributing to industry and society alike.
